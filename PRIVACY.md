# SelStats Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 30, 2026_

SelStats is a browser extension published by Somerled.Design.

**SelStats does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data.**

- All calculations (Count, Sum, Average, Median, Min, Max) happen locally in your browser, on the text you select or the table column you Alt/Option-click.
- SelStats makes no network requests and contains no analytics, tracking, or remote code.
- The only thing stored is your preferences (which stats to copy and the copy format), saved with Chrome's `storage` API. These never leave your browser except through Chrome's own sync if you have it enabled.
- Page content is read only to compute the stats shown, and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Questions: support@somerled.design · https://github.com/SomerledDesign/SelStats
